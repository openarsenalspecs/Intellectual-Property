# Audiovista Specification

**Cinema, Perfected by AI.**

Audiovista is an open-source specification for building next-generation AI-powered cinematic production systems. It defines a modular architecture for synchronizing dialogue, sound effects, ambient audio, music, visuals, physics, and storytelling into a unified production pipeline. Rather than focusing solely on video generation, Audiovista establishes a comprehensive framework for intelligent audiovisual creation, enabling developers to build systems capable of producing immersive, cinematic experiences with consistent quality and realism.

The specification is designed to remain model agnostic, allowing implementations to integrate any compatible AI models for video generation, speech synthesis, music composition, visual effects, or physics simulation. Every subsystem is modular, replaceable, and independently extensible.
